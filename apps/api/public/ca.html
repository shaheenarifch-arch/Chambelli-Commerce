Help & questions

Where do orders ship from?

Every item in this store is held in a warehouse in Canada, so your order never crosses the border and you pay no customs duties or brokerage fees.

Is shipping really included?

Yes. The price you see is in Canadian dollars and already includes shipping anywhere we deliver in Canada. You get a tracking number by email once your order ships.

Will I be charged sales tax?

When GST/HST applies, it is added at checkout at your province's rate and shown before you pay. You can check the rate for your province on the Canada Revenue Agency GST/HST calculator.

What do I need at checkout?

A Canadian shipping address with your province and a valid postal code (for example M5V 2T6). You can pay by card or PayPal; we never see or store your card number.

How do I return something?

Contact us with your order number and the reason, and we will send you return instructions. Email agents@getservices.ai.
