Help & questions

Where do orders ship from?

Every item in this store is held in a warehouse in Germany, so your order never goes through customs and you pay no import VAT or handling fees on delivery.

Where do you deliver?

For now we deliver to addresses in Germany only. The price you see is in euros and already includes delivery. You get a tracking number by email once your order ships.

Does the price include VAT?

Yes. Any VAT due is already in the price shown, so nothing is added at checkout. Learn more on Your Europe: prices and payments.

What do I need at checkout?

A delivery address in Germany with a valid five-digit postcode (Postleitzahl, for example 10115). You can pay by card or PayPal; we never see or store your card number.

How do I return something?

Under EU consumer law you can withdraw from most online orders within 14 days of delivery. Email us your order number and the reason and we will send return instructions: agents@getservices.ai. Help: Your Europe: returns.

How is my data handled?

We use your details only to deliver your order and answer your questions, as required by the GDPR. Ask us to see or delete your data at agents@getservices.ai. Help: Your Europe: data protection.
