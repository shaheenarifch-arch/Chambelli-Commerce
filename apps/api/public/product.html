Help & questions

How do I choose a size?

Pick a size or option from the list above the price. Sizes follow the seller's chart, so if you are between two sizes we suggest the larger one. Unsure? Email us before you buy and we will check the measurements for you.

How does checkout work?

Add the item to your cart and press Checkout. Checkout has three steps: Delivery (where we should send it), Payment (card or PayPal) and Place order. Your order summary stays on screen the whole time, so you can change quantities or remove items before you pay. Card payments, including Apple Pay and Google Pay where your device offers them, are handled by Stripe; PayPal payments by PayPal. We never see or store your card number. Learn more: Stripe Support, PayPal Help Center.

Is delivery included? When will it arrive?

Yes. The price shown already includes standard delivery, so nothing is added for shipping at checkout. Orders usually arrive in 5 to 10 business days; checkout shows the expected dates. You get a tracking number by email once your order ships.

How do I return something?

Email us your order number and the reason and we will send return instructions: agents@getservices.ai.
