Help & questions

How do I choose a size?

Pick a size or option from the list above the price. Sizes follow the seller's chart, so if you are between two sizes we suggest the larger one. Unsure? Email us before you buy and we will check the measurements for you.

How does checkout work?

Press Buy now (or add items to your cart and press Checkout), enter your delivery address, then press Continue to PayPal and confirm on PayPal's secure page. Nothing is charged until you confirm there. Tick "Remember my details" to skip typing next time. You can pay with a PayPal account or as a guest with a debit or credit card; we never see or store your card number. Learn more: PayPal Help Center.

Is delivery included?

Yes. The price shown already includes standard delivery, so nothing is added for shipping at checkout. You get a tracking number by email once your order ships.

How do I return something?

Email us your order number and the reason and we will send return instructions: agents@getservices.ai.
