Help & questions

Where do orders ship from?

Every item in this store is held in a warehouse in the UK, so your order never goes through customs and you pay no import VAT or handling fees on delivery.

Is delivery really included?

Yes. The price you see is in pounds and already includes delivery anywhere we deliver in the UK. You get a tracking number by email once your order ships.

Does the price include VAT?

Yes. Any VAT due is already in the price shown, so nothing is added at checkout. Learn more on GOV.UK: VAT for businesses.

What do I need at checkout?

A UK delivery address with a valid postcode (for example SW1A 1AA). You can pay by card or PayPal; we never see or store your card number.

How do I return something?

You can cancel most online orders within 14 days of delivery under UK consumer law. Email us your order number and the reason and we will send return instructions: agents@getservices.ai. Help: Citizens Advice: returns.
